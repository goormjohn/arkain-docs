***

### Schedule

* **Oct 7, 2026**: New membership subscriptions and credit purchases will be restricted

* All data, including containers and files, will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered after the service is discontinued.

### Refunds&#x20;

* **Automatic refund**: Remaining credit and the unused portion of your paid Membership as of the discontinuation date will be automatically refunded to your original payment method—no action is required.
* **Want it sooner?** Contact us via chat or email to have your refund processed earlier.&#x20;
* **If a card refund isn't possible**: We'll contact you individually to arrange a bank transfer. Any fees for international bank transfers will be covered by Arkain.

### Privacy Policy Update

Arkain's Privacy Policy will be updated to include bank account information as a newly collected item for refund processing, **effective October 7, 2026**. Please see the updated Privacy Policy for details.
